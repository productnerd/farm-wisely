🫒 Recommended shoot order

Sorted by what each farm looks like right now. Confirm harvest dates on each call.

  1. Day 1 · Περβόλι + Planty · ⏰ film by Fri 6 Nov. Coastal olives are picked first, and milling fresh oil is the money shot. Planty is indoors, so it rides along.
  2. Day 2 · Ygea + Akaki Grove · ⏰ film by Fri 13 Nov, while the olives are on the trees. Olive harvest and hens at Ygea. Early citrus may be colouring at Akaki too.
  3. Day 3 · ALION · ⏰ film by Fri 13 Nov. ALION works any time.
  4. Day 4 · Mountain Berries · ⏰ film by Fri 20 Nov. The last autumn colour and any late raspberries go with the first frosts at 1,300 m. If nothing is left, film the people and landscape and get summer footage, or move to May to June.
  5. Day 5 · Όρος Μαχαιρά · ⏰ film by Fri 27 Nov. Carob flowers in autumn keep bees foraging on warm days. Book a still, sunny day around midday, before December cold.
  6. Day 6 · Α/φοί Ανδρέου · ⏰ film by Fri 4 Dec. No season. Last for biosecurity, and it gives time to confirm the CAP link with IMH.
